Before booking

    Terms used here

    240-hour rule
    China lets New Zealanders in without a visa for up to 10 days if they arrive from one country and leave to a different one (for example Korea in, Hong Kong out).
    Nonstop where possible
    Swaps in a nonstop or full-service flight on each leg where one exists. It costs more but avoids long layovers and budget-airline bag limits.
    Budget airlines
    Jetstar, Zipair, Spring, Jin Air, Air Seoul, Thai Lion, Peach and Air Busan fares include a cabin bag only (often 7 kg).
