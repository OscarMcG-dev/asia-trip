Booking plan: with Casper, then Hong Kong

For you and Ina, budget rooms. Seoul 16–23 Dec, Beijing 23–27, Shanghai 27–30, Suzhou 30 Dec–2 Jan, Hong Kong 2–5 Jan. Ranked in the order to book, most urgent first. Tick each one off as you book it; the ticks are saved on this device only.

Before you start

  • Both passports to hand. Enter names exactly as printed, given names and surname. China and Korea check passports against every ticket and hotel.
  • Passports must be valid for at least 6 months after 5 Jan.
  • Install the 12306 app (China Railway, English version), register with your passport and add Ina as a passenger. Verification can take a day, so do it before 13 Dec.
  • Install Alipay and link a Visa or Mastercard. Metro, taxis and most shops in China use it.
  • Tell Casper which flight and trains you're booking so you end up on the same ones.

Before you fly

  • Korea: fill in the e-Arrival card online before landing on 16 Dec. New Zealanders don't need a K-ETA until 31 Dec 2026.
  • China (23 Dec): you enter on 240-hour visa-free transit. At check-in in Seoul and at Beijing immigration, say "240-hour transit" and show the Futian → West Kowloon train ticket for 2 Jan as your onward ticket. Keep the HKG → AKL booking and hotel bookings handy too. Print them as well as having them on your phone.
  • China exit (2 Jan): you must be across the border into Hong Kong by midnight on 2 Jan.
  • An eSIM that covers Korea, mainland China and Hong Kong (a foreign eSIM in China gives normal internet).
  • Travel insurance for both of you.
